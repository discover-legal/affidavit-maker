  The bug: after ingesting a court paper, the life-story page says the user
  married themself. Compare the two screenshots I sent — before ingestion it
  reads "You married Alex Example"; after, it reads "You married Jordan S. 
  Example" (the user's own name), and the spouse figure in the family portrait
  is relabeled "Jordan".

  Root cause, confirmed in the stored profile row and the code:

  1. The ingested paper's kind comes back as "Original petition served on you",
  which matches the served-on-you regex in app/api/profile/ingest/route.ts:299,
  so the route flips role to 'respondent' (route.ts:301) — reasonable on its
  own.
  2. But it leaves the party names untouched: the profile still has
  petitionerName: "Jordan S. Example" (the user, from the interview where they
  were petitioner) and respondentName: "Alex Example".
  3. components/app/LifeStoryClient.tsx:983-986 derives self/spouse from role:
  respondent → spouse = petitionerName. With the stale names, spouse resolves to
  the user's own name. The fix-my-story edit form has the same problem in
  reverse — it would preload "Your name" with Alex.

  A related inconsistency: HearingPrepClient.tsx:260 (spouseFullName) is
  role-blind — it always returns respondentName. So today it accidentally shows
  the right spouse for this profile, but if the role flip ever swapped the names
  properly, hearing prep would then be wrong. The components disagree on how
  self/spouse are derived from the party fields.

  Suggested direction (not applied — you asked for a review): when the ingest
  flips role to respondent and petitionerName matches the user's affiantName,
  swap the petitioner/respondent name fields in the same update — or better,
  store spouseName as its own field and derive petitioner/respondent from role
  at render time, so every component agrees. The e2e driver should also assert
  the spouse's name still appears after ingestion; it currently only checks for
  "Filed"/"Served" on the timeline, which is why 21/21 passes over this.
